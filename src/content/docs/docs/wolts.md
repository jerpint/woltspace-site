Plus a 🐶 dog for routing messages from Telegram and Slack, and a 🐺 wolf that runs every wolt's schedules.

## What you get

- **Split view** - terminal on the left, viewport on the right. The wolt works, results appear live. Everything through one URL.
- **Persistent memory** - identity, context, learnings. Session 42 picks up where 41 left off. Stop and resume conversations at will.
- **Apps** - full-stack apps with their own server and dependencies. Served via subdomain proxy. Shareable via tunnel.
- **Messaging** - chat from Telegram or Slack. The wolt spawns sessions, does real work, and notifies you when done.
- **Scheduling** - the wolf runs cron jobs for every wolt. Daily digests, automated reviews, morning playlists - each wolt owns its own schedule.
- **Sandboxed** - everything runs in a Docker container. Safe to let the wolt work autonomously. Your data stays local.
